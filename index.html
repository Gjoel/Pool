Desk rules

Print cards

A4 sheets of 10, each 80 × 48 mm for a 54 × 86 mm pouch. Print at 100% on card stock, cut at the corner marks, laminate.

The first number is the next one after the last sheet printed or the highest card in use, whichever is higher. Double-sided prints the brand on the front and the barcode on the back of the same sheet; set the printer to print on both sides and match the flip setting here. The small number on the front should match the barcode behind it. Standard cards are numbered from 100001, family cards from 400001 (the child barcode on each is the card number plus 50000) and corporate cards from 150001. On a family card the adult barcode sits in the top-left corner and the child barcode in the bottom-right, diagonally opposite each other, so the scanner can only ever catch one of them at a time.

Same number, same balance. Also available from the member's screen.

Guest balance check

A separate page where a pass holder types their card number and phone number and sees entries left and the expiry date. Nothing else is shown.

Page address: . Off means the page answers every request with "not switched on"; nothing about any card is reachable until the box is ticked.

Import a member list

A CSV with a header row. Recognised columns (any order, any case): name, phone, kind, remaining, barcode, expires_on, note, rate, email. Kind is adult, senior, family or account (default adult). Remaining becomes an opening balance. Cards are assigned later at the desk, or now if the barcode column is filled.

Export

Every CSV now lives on the Reports tab, together with the statements and the lists to act on.
