Desk rules

Print cards

A4 sheets of 10, each 80 × 48 mm for a 54 × 86 mm pouch. Print at 100% on card stock, cut at the corner marks, laminate.

The first number is the next one after the last sheet printed or the highest card in use, whichever is higher. Double-sided prints the brand on the front and the barcode on the back of the same sheet; set the printer to print on both sides and match the flip setting here. The small number on the front should match the barcode behind it.

Import a member list

A CSV with a header row. Recognised columns (any order, any case): name, phone, kind, remaining, barcode, expires_on, note, rate, email. Kind is adult, senior, family, resident or account (default adult). Remaining becomes an opening balance. Cards are assigned later at the desk, or now if the barcode column is filled.
